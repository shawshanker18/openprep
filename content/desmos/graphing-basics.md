Desmos draws anything you type that starts with `y=` (or `x=`). Each expression has its own colour.

**Enter:** $y = 2x + 1$ and $y = x^2 - 3$.

- **Click a point on a curve** to see its coordinates. Grey dots mark the important points: intercepts, vertices, and intersections.
- **Zoom** with the mouse wheel, or the + and − buttons. If a graph seems to vanish, zoom out. It is usually just off the screen.
- **Type `^` for exponents** and `sqrt` for $\sqrt{\ }$. Desmos turns `/` into a fraction automatically.

**On the test:** whenever a question gives you an equation and asks about its graph (intercepts, where it's positive, its highest point), type it in first and look before doing any algebra.
