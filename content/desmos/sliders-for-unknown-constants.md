---
title: Sliders for unknown constants
order: 7
expressions: ["y=x^2+kx+9", "k=6"]
---
When an equation contains an unknown constant, Desmos offers a **slider** for it. Drag the slider and watch the graph change.

**Try it:** $y = x^2 + kx + 9$ is loaded with $k = 6$. At $k = 6$ the parabola just touches the $x$ axis, which means exactly one solution. Drag $k$ and watch it cross the axis twice, or not at all.

- To add a slider yourself, type an equation with a new letter (like $k$) and click **add slider**.
- Click the slider's ends to set its range and step size.

**On the test:** "for what value of $k$ does the equation have exactly one solution / no solution?" Slide until the graph does what the question describes, then check with the discriminant if you have time.
