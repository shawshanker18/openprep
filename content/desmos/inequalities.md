Type an inequality and Desmos shades every point that makes it true. Where two shadings overlap is the solution region of the system.

**Try it:** $y < 2x + 1$ and $y \ge -x + 4$ are loaded. The darker overlapping area is where both hold.

- A **dashed** boundary line means the line itself is not included ($<$ or $>$). A **solid** line means it is ($\le$ or $\ge$).
- To test an answer choice like $(3, 2)$, add the point `(3,2)` and see whether it lands in the overlap.

**On the test:** for "which point is a solution to the system?", graph the system and plot each answer choice.
