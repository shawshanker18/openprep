GeoGebra computes statistics for a list in one line.

**Try it:** the list $L = [12, 15, 15, 18, 21, 40]$ is loaded. GeoGebra shows the **mean** is $20.1\overline{6}$ and the **median** is $16.5$.

- The mean is pulled up by the outlier $40$; the median is not. Questions often ask which measure changes more when a value is removed. Check by deleting $40$ from the list.
- Other useful functions: `stdev(L)`, `min(L)`, `max(L)`, `total(L)`.

**On the test:** for data sets given as lists or dot plots, type the values in instead of adding by hand.
